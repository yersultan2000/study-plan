        Methods

        What the research says works, and how to use it for each goal.

        1
        Test yourself, don't rereadPractice testing and spaced practice are the two techniques rated most effective in the big 2013 review of study methods (Dunlosky et al.). Rereading and highlighting rated low.
        2
        Space it outReview on day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30. Cramming feels productive but fades fast.
        3
        Every mistake goes in the logYour score rises by fixing repeat mistakes, not by doing more questions.
        4
        Practise exactly like the real thingTimed, full length, no phone, real conditions, every week.
